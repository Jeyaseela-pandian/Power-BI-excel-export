Export a full report page to Excel in one click

Every chart, table, KPI and active filter, laid out like your report, formatted and ready to filter, sort and share.

Read-only access. Your report data goes straight from Power BI to your browser and never reaches our servers.

Whole page, one file

Export the current page or every page of a report. Each page becomes a sheet laid out like the report.

Real Excel tables

Numbers stay numbers, with formats. Every visual is an Excel table with its own filter and sort buttons.

Filters on record

Slicer selections and report filters are written at the top of each sheet, so everyone knows what the numbers show.
